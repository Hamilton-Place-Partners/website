Where to start

When the organization becomes the constraint

“We're growing faster than our organization.”

Decisions pile up with a few people, teams are stretched, and hiring can’t keep up. We sort out who owns what and who decides, and redesign the work so you can grow capacity without growing headcount at the same rate.

“We have to cut costs without breaking things.”

Cutting headcount alone rarely holds. We start with the work: what can be eliminated, simplified or automated, and how roles need to change around it. You get a lower cost base that still performs, and keep the people you can’t afford to lose, with a clear role in what comes next.

“We've invested in AI, but the work hasn't changed.”

The tools are live and the pilots look promising, but roles, responsibilities and performance expectations haven’t changed. We redesign how people and AI agents share the work: what gets delegated, who decides, where human oversight is needed and who stays accountable. And we prepare managers to lead it: setting the quality bar, knowing when to challenge AI output, and making sure people keep building the judgment to use it well.

“Our HR team has the plan, but not the capacity for this.”

A carve-out, a restructuring or a fast scale-up can double the load on an HR team overnight. We join your HR leadership as a senior partner for the transformation: leading the people workstreams, guiding managers through the change and keeping the people side on pace with the deal or the plan. Then we hand back to your team.

People and AI

Your tech team decides what AI can do. We design how the organization works with it.

We redesign how work gets done, decisions are made, managers lead and people develop, so your investment in AI translates into better business performance.

Roles and accountability

We clarify what people own, what AI agents can handle and who is responsible for the result. We update roles and career paths as the work changes.

Managers with capacity to lead

We shape team sizes and management responsibilities around the oversight, coaching and decision-making AI-enabled work requires, so managers have time to lead.

A pipeline of future talent

When AI takes over work that used to teach junior employees, we create new ways for people to build experience, develop judgment and prepare for greater responsibility.

Ways of working that evolve

We help teams agree how AI is used, when people need to step in, how outputs are checked and how practices adapt as the technology improves.
